### *Jailbreaking was never meant to be paid.*

***

To whoever is reading this,

If you're here, you probably know the feeling. You remember the golden days—spending hours on forums, tweaking your springboard, finding that one perfect theme, and sharing it with a community that was built on curiosity, not cash flow. 

Somewhere along the line, things changed. The scene got flooded with paywalls, subscription models for simple tweaks, and DRM that makes you jump through hoops just to customize the device you already own. 

I'm making this repo because I’m tired of that. 

**Jailbreaking was never meant to be paid.** It was meant to be free. It was about breaking the chains, not putting a price tag on them. It was meant to be open, accessible, and shared by people who just love messing with their tech.

This repo is my small corner of the internet to keep that spirit alive. Here, you won't find hidden fees, locked features, or "premium" tiers. You'll just find the stuff I make, the themes I tweak, and the packages I want to share with you. It’s free, it’s open, and it’s for the love of the game.

Use it, enjoy it, break your device, and learn how to fix it. That's what this is all about.

Stay free.

**— ssxfien**
